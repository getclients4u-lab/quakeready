The problem

You felt it. Your heart raced. Then… you froze.

That freeze is completely normal — and completely fixable. The problem isn't that you don't care. It's that nobody ever taught you what to actually do, and the "advice" you've heard is half wrong.

Myth #1 "Stand in a doorway." Modern doorways aren't stronger than the room — and the swinging door is a weapon.
Myth #2 "Run outside." The #1 cause of quake injury is being hit by falling objects inside — while people sprint through them.
Myth #3 "The Triangle of Life." Not supported by search-and-rescue data. Global agencies all teach the same thing: drop, cover, hold on.
