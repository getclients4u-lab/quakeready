# The 12-Week Quake-Ready Tracker™

## Turn the System Into a 10-Minute-Per-Week Habit — With a Family Duty Roster

**Why this worksheet exists:** Every preparedness system fails the same way: it gets
done once, feels great for a month, and quietly decays. Water expires unrotated,
kids forget the drill, the go-bag becomes a bag of expired granola bars. This tracker
turns QuakeReady™ into a maintained habit with assigned jobs, so it survives real life.

**How to run it:** Assign a "Quake Captain" for the week (rotate through household
members — kids old enough to participate should take turns too). Each week has one
small task, 10 minutes or less. Check it off. Rotate the captain. Repeat.

---

## Weeks 1–4: Foundation Month

**Week 1 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Complete the Home Hazard Hunt (Tool #2) if not already done
- [ ] Confirm gas/water shutoff locations are known by every adult

**Week 2 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Anchor the top 3 HIGH-priority items from the Hazard Hunt
- [ ] Locate/store the shutoff wrench at the gas valve

**Week 3 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Build/complete the 72-Hour Go-Bag (Tool #3) for every household member
- [ ] Confirm the car kit is stocked

**Week 4 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Run the full Drop, Cover, Hold On Drill (Tool #4), timed, in 3 rooms
- [ ] Complete the Family Emergency Plan (Tool #5) — all sections filled in

---

## Weeks 5–8: Reinforcement Month

**Week 5 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Post the Aftershock & Post-Quake Safety Card (Tool #6) somewhere visible
- [ ] Confirm the out-of-area contact still agrees to the role and has your info

**Week 6 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Check water storage — any cloudiness or off-smell? Rotate if needed
- [ ] Confirm all go-bag flashlights have working batteries

**Week 7 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Review the Work/Car/Travel Playbook (Tool #7) — has anyone changed jobs,
      schools, or routines since the last review?
- [ ] Test the battery/hand-crank radio

**Week 8 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Send the practice check-in text to your out-of-area contact (unannounced,
      as part of a surprise mini-drill)
- [ ] Check prescription medication dates in the go-bags

---

## Weeks 9–12: Mastery Month

**Week 9 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Run a surprise Drop, Cover, Hold On drill — no warning, see the real reaction time
- [ ] Re-check any anchoring hardware installed in Week 2 — still secure?

**Week 10 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Review the Family Emergency Plan with any household changes (new school year,
      new job, new pet) — update as needed
- [ ] Confirm cash reserve in go-bags is still adequate

**Week 11 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Full go-bag re-check: water dates, food dates, batteries, meds — top 3 items
      most commonly found expired
- [ ] Ask kids to recite the family plan back in their own words

**Week 12 — Captain: __________**
- [ ] Run the full Drop, Cover, Hold On Drill one more time, in a 4th location
      (outdoors or car) to close out the quarter
- [ ] **Full system review:** score yourself using the Mastery Scorecard below,
      then restart the cycle at Week 1 for the next quarter

## Pro Tips From the Field

1. **Tie the weekly task to an existing habit** — Sunday grocery trip, trash day,
   whatever already happens weekly. Piggyback the reminder onto it.
2. **Let kids be Quake Captain.** Ownership of even a small task (checking flashlight
   batteries) builds real retention of the whole system.
3. **The surprise drills matter more than the scheduled ones.** Real events don't
   announce themselves — Weeks 9 is deliberately unannounced for this reason.
4. **Don't skip weeks — shrink them instead.** A 2-minute version of the task beats
   skipping it entirely; the habit is the point, not perfection.
5. **Screenshot or photograph your Week 12 scorecard** each quarter — watching the
   score improve over a year is real, visible proof the system is working.
